Things I Learned This Year

These are the small lessons and big shifts that have stuck with me. The kind of things that feel important not because they’re impressive, but because they changed how I see my days.

  • It’s okay to start something without knowing exactly where it will go.
  • Progress is a series of tiny, imperfect steps — not one big leap.
  • Creativity often comes from making space for curiosity, not forcing answers.
  • Learning in public helps me stay honest and keeps me motivated.
  • Being kind to myself makes it easier to keep trying.

Every one of these is still a work in progress. That’s the whole point.
